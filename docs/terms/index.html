3. What the pre-assessment is

The pre-assessment is a Bayesian adaptive self-administered tool that produces an estimated AI Posture (formally Aggregated Intelligence Posture) for your organization based on your answers. The output is labeled an estimate, not a verified assertion.

4. What the pre-assessment is not

  • Not a certification. No seal. No attestation of compliance.
  • Not an audit. No independent verification. No evidence collection.
  • Not legal advice. The Regulation vector discusses obligations. It does not tell you how to meet them for your jurisdiction. Consult qualified counsel for legal questions.
  • Not a substitute for per-vector measurement. Verified assertions require the reference implementations named in the framework documentation.
  • Not a scoring leaderboard. No public directory of scores is published. Only aggregate statistics, without attribution.
  • Not a sales qualifier. Low estimates do not gate access to reference products. High estimates do not unlock anything.
