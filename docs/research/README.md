# Research notes

These notes are non-normative. They support positioning, validation, and contributor review. The specification remains the source of truth for AI Posture requirements.

## Use this index

- [Design basis and open questions](design-basis-open-questions.md): why the current framework design is plausible, what is provisional, and what would trigger revision.
- [Validation backlog](validation-backlog.md): testable questions for construct validity, reviewer agreement, cross-vector comparability, freshness, weakest-link validity, stakeholder interpretation, and vector sufficiency.
- [Weakest-link and level-semantics stress tests](weakest-link-stress-tests.md): edge cases for minimum-vector aggregation and shared level names.
- [Adjacent-framework crosswalk](adjacent-framework-crosswalk.md): how AI Posture relates to governance, risk, compliance, maturity, and behavior-change models without claiming replacement.

## Boundaries

Do not treat these notes as scoring rules.

Do not use them to claim certification, legal advice, audit output, or verified AI Posture.

Do use them to decide whether a proposed change belongs in copy, PRD, SPEC.md, INTENT.md, or CHANGELOG.md.
